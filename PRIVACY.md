# Privacy Policy for Mica Translate

**Last updated:** 09.09.2026

Thank you for using Mica Translate! We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how your data is handled.

## 1. Data collection

Mica Translate does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. We do not use analytics, advertising, or require registration.

### Text you translate

When you translate text, it is sent to the **translation provider** you have selected (Google, Yandex, Bing, etc.) through the open‑source GTranslate library. This text is required to perform the translation and is **not stored** by the app or on any servers operated by us (we do not have our own servers).

## 2. Local settings

All application settings (hotkey, default language, selected provider, etc.) are stored **locally on your computer** in the app’s standard data folder. They are never transmitted over the network.

## 3. Internet access

The app requires an internet connection to perform translations. Without it, translation will not work. The app sends only the text you want to translate to the provider’s API.

## 4. Third-party services

We use the **GTranslate** library, which accesses unofficial APIs of the following services:

- Google Translate
- Yandex Translate
- Microsoft Translator (Bing)
- and others available in GTranslate

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/)
- [Microsoft Privacy Policy](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement)

Please note that your text is processed by these services according to their terms.

## 5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be available in the repository or on the Microsoft Store page. By continuing to use the app after changes, you agree to the updated policy.
